# Anatomy — Brand

Adaptation for VOX based on the branding brief supplied by the user. This is an original workflow, not an official Kaffi Studio skill.

1. Define the brand decision. Capture what the business sells, who buys it, their alternatives, and what the brand must help them understand or do. Separate supplied evidence from assumptions. If there is no business or product brief, ask for it before proposing names.
2. Write the positioning: audience, buying situation, category, promise, and credible reasons to believe. Flag the claims that need validation. Do not invent interviews, competitors, metrics, or market research.
3. Explore distinct naming and visual territories privately. Compare fit to positioning, memorability, pronunciation, and how well the direction works across touchpoints. Show only the two or three strongest, each with a distinct premise, name candidates, voice, palette, type direction, and one practical example. Recommend one and explain the tradeoff. Do not claim trademark or domain availability without a verified check.
4. Let the user choose a direction. Until then, label all identity ideas as concepts. When the user selects a direction, develop that one into a practical guide: positioning and message hierarchy; naming and spelling; tone with do/don't examples; logo concept and usage; accessible color pairs with hex values; type hierarchy and fallbacks; photography or illustration; and examples for two channels the team uses.
5. Finish with decisions that are ready, decisions that still need review, and the files or assets the team needs next. Describe proposed assets as proposals unless actual files were produced.

For a six-week engagement plan when requested, map discovery, positioning, exploration, selection, refinement, and handoff to weekly milestones. Treat the duration as an estimate.
